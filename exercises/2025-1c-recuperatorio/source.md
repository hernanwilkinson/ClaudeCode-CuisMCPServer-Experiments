# Source

- Original directory: `2025-1c/Recuperatorio/` in the parciales repository.
- Statement: `2025-1C-Recu1-Práctica.pdf` (copied as `spec-original.pdf`). `2025-1C-Recu-Teórica.pdf` is the theory exam and was skipped.

## starting/

- `starting/2025-1C-Recu.st`: the initial code, i.e. the course's solution of the first midterm translated into Spanish (category `2025-1C-Recu`): `EscapeDeFlota`, `NaveEspacial`, `Propulsores` (`Gravitones`, `ImpulsoEstandar`, `VelocidadLuz`), `SectorEspacial` (`Asteroides`, `Nebulosa`, `Planetario`) and `EscapeDeFlotaTests` (20 tests). It is essentially the same code as `2025-1c-parcial1/solution/ISW-Parcial1-1C25-Solu.st`, re-categorized.

## solution/

- `solution/2025-1C-Recu1-Ptos123.st`: solution of points 1 to 3 (cracked tank as `TanqueAntimateria` with `Intacto`/`Rajado`, overheated warp as `VelocidadLuzSobrecalentada`, blink and stranded-sector index).
- `solution/2025-1C-Recu1-Pto4.st`: solution of point 4 (`NaveNodriza` and `NaveTactica` as subclasses of `NaveEspacial`, plus `NaveNodrizaTests`).
  The two solutions are independent of each other (each starts from the initial code), matching the exam rule that a student did either points 1-3 or point 4.
