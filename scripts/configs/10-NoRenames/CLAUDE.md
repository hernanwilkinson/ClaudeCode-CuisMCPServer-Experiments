## Do not rename anything

Keep every class, selector, instance variable and temporary name exactly as it is given: do
not rename any of them, and do not replace an existing name with a new one that means the
same. Improve the design by changing the structure only (extract, inline, move, push up, push
down, replace conditionals with polymorphism, remove duplication) with the names as they are.
New classes and methods you add may be named as you see fit.
